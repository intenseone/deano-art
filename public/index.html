About Deano

Dean Greenberg grew up in Saint Paul, Minnesota. He started out in the scrap business, moved into meat processing, and went on to run Newport Cold Storage in Saint Paul. He has also backed many startups as an investor.

He has collected art for 50 years. His collection includes Joan Miró and Alexander Calder, along with many sculptures. A few years ago he picked up a set of markers and started making his own.

He works at home with no plan. He starts with a line and lets the piece find its shape, and he calls every one of them a doodle. The collector shows in the work: heavy black lines, floating shapes and flat, bright color that recall Miró and Calder. He moves between marker, colored pencil and paint, and he keeps trying new materials.

Deano has never shown his work in a gallery. He makes art because it is therapeutic and because it lets him explore new worlds. Most pieces go to family and friends, and many arrive by text message.
