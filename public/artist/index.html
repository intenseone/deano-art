Hi Dean. Turn each piece so it’s the right way up, give it a name if you like, and tick Don’t show on any piece you want off the website. Everything saves by itself. Press Publish to website when you’re ready.

Add photos of new art. Each photo is straightened and cropped to the paper, then shows up at the top of the gallery in about 3 minutes. After that, come back to the first tab to turn it and give it a name.

For the best photo
  • Lay the art flat on a dark table or the black board.
  • Hold the phone straight above it so the whole page fits with a little dark border around it.
  • Use daylight or a bright room, and keep your shadow off the paper.
